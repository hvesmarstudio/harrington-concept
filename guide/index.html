The landscape

Where people buy pre-owned
tech today.

Described in general, publicly verifiable terms. Sources on the final page. Harrington doesn't need to out-shout anyone, just out-prove them.

REGIONAL SPECIALIST

CompAsia

Sells second-hand devices in Malaysia online, with a strong focus on Apple and Samsung, leading with warranty and device-check messaging.

Lesson: warranty and checks are now table stakes for the category.

GLOBAL MARKETPLACE

Back Market

Founded in Paris in 2014; a marketplace connecting buyers with vetted professional refurbishers. Its grades describe cosmetic condition, with devices guaranteed fully functional.

Lesson: a clear, named grading system makes refurbished feel mainstream.

PEER-TO-PEER

Carousell & Mudah.my

Classifieds where individuals list their own devices. Huge choice and bargains, but testing, honesty and after-sales depend entirely on each seller.

Lesson: the demand for value is real; the risk is the gap.

NEW RETAIL

Brand stores & resellers

Brand-new devices with manufacturer warranty and total certainty about condition, at full price.

Lesson: "as good as new" is the experience to match, at a better price.

Harrington's gap

A per-device inspection report + Score ID, two separate grades (function and cosmetics, not one blended label), specialist depth in e-readers, cameras and drones, and a warmer, local voice.

Functional grade · How it works

A, B or C: a performance
standard, not a vibe.

Proposed standards. Battery thresholds and test lists to be validated with Harrington's technicians and set per category.

AFunctionalPERFORMS LIKE NEW
TestingPasses 100% of the category checklist.
BatteryProposed ≥ 85% of design capacity, where relevant.
ComponentsOriginal or manufacturer-equivalent parts; no faults.
LimitationsNone.

For: buyers who want new-like performance, e.g. a creator's main camera.

BFunctionalFULLY WORKING
TestingPasses every core function test.
BatteryProposed ≥ 80% of design capacity, where relevant.
ComponentsAny replaced part is disclosed on the report.
LimitationsMinor, non-core, disclosed (e.g. slightly slower charging).

For: everyday users who want reliable value, e.g. a student laptop.

CFunctionalWORKS, WITH NOTES
TestingAll core functions work for everyday use.
BatteryProposed ≥ 70%, or a replacement noted, where relevant.
ComponentsWear or replacements disclosed.
LimitationsOne or more listed clearly (e.g. an older model past OS updates).

For: budget or secondary devices, e.g. a kid's first tablet.

Below Grade C's standard, a device is not sold as Harrington Certified. Every grade carries a warranty; disclosed limitations are stated on the report.

Cosmetic grade · How it looks

Pristine, Clean or Lived-in.

Graded separately from function. A Lived-in phone can be Grade A, and it might be the smartest buy on the site.

Pristine

No visible signs of use at arm's length. At most, faint micro-marks under bright light. Looks new out of the box.

Photos: front, back, edges. Gift-ready.

Clean

Light signs of use. Fine hairlines or small marks visible on close inspection; no screen marks visible when the display is on.

Photos: every noted mark, labelled.

Lived-in

Visible wear with character. Scuffs, scratches or small dents that don't affect function (that's the Functional Grade's job).

Photos: close-ups of each mark, so there are no surprises.

A+ Lived-in = works like new, priced for wear
B+ Pristine = looks new, minor limits disclosed
A+ Pristine = the closest thing to new
